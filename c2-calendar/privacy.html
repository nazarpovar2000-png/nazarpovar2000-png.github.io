C2 Calendar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

C2 Calendar is a private family calendar app operated by Nazar Povar for his family. This page explains what data the app uses.

Account data

When you create an account we store your email address and a hashed password with our hosting provider (Supabase). Calendar items, tasks, lists, notes, recipes and photos you add are stored there and are visible only to members of your family group.

Google data

If you choose to connect a Google account, the app requests access to Google Calendar and Gmail so it can show your calendars and events, create and edit events you ask it to, show your inbox and unread counts, open messages, and send or reply to emails you write in the app. Google data is fetched on demand to display it to you and is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The app stores only the OAuth refresh token (to keep the connection), your Google email address and your calendar display choices. Message contents and events are not stored on our servers.

C2 Calendar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deletion

You can disconnect a Google account at any time in Settings → Google accounts, which deletes the stored token. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete your account and all data, email nazar.povar.2000@gmail.com.
