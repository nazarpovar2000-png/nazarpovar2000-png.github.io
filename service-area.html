Service Area

Where we work

We are based in Everett and work within roughly 40 miles — covering most of Snohomish County and the northern half of King County.

We are a service-area business: our crew comes to you. There is no showroom to visit, which keeps our overhead low and our prices honest. Estimates are free anywhere in the area below.

If you are just outside the list, call anyway — we sometimes take projects slightly farther out depending on the schedule.

Everett, WA

Our home base. From the older homes around downtown and Bayside to newer construction in Silver Lake and Valley View, we have worked on almost every kind of house Everett has.

Mukilteo, WA

Salt air and wind-driven rain make exterior maintenance a constant here. Decks, siding, and fencing get extra attention to fasteners and flashing.

Lynnwood, WA

A lot of 1960s and 70s homes with original flooring, cabinets, and decks that are due for an update. Kitchen and flooring work is common here.

Edmonds, WA

Older character homes near the waterfront bowl. Nothing is square, which is exactly where good carpentry shows.

Marysville, WA

Newer subdivisions and larger lots. Deck building, fencing, and full backyard improvements are frequent requests.

Snohomish, WA

Rural properties, outbuildings, and long fence runs. We handle fence lines on acreage.

Monroe, WA

Growing fast, with a mix of new builds and older farmhouses. Siding, decks, and repair work.

Mill Creek, WA

HOA-heavy neighborhoods where material choice and approval matter. We work with HOA requirements.

Bothell, WA

A mix of older and newer housing stock, plus a lot of townhomes where space is tight and precision matters.

Lake Stevens, WA

Lakefront and near-lake homes where moisture and drainage drive most exterior problems.

Arlington, WA

Larger properties and outbuildings. Fencing, decks, and structural repairs.

Shoreline, WA

Older mid-century homes with original layouts. Bathroom and kitchen remodels are the most common projects.

Mountlake Terrace, WA

Compact lots and older homes. Efficient remodels and repair work.

Woodinville, WA

Higher-end homes where finish carpentry, built-ins, and detail work matter most.

Kirkland & Redmond, WA

We travel here for larger remodel projects, particularly kitchens, bathrooms, and built-ins.

North Seattle, WA

We take select projects in north Seattle — typically remodels where a small dedicated crew is an advantage.

Frequently asked questions

Do you charge for travel?

No. Estimates are free anywhere in our service area, and there is no travel surcharge within roughly 40 miles of Everett.

Do you work outside this list?

Sometimes. Call and ask — if the project fits our schedule we can often stretch the radius a bit.

Are you licensed to work in King County?

Yes. We are a licensed and insured Washington contractor and work throughout both Snohomish and King County.
