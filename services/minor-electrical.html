Minor electrical work

Outlets, switches, fixtures and ceiling fans — installed safely, with a licensed electrician where the code requires one.

We replace outlets, switches, dimmers, light fixtures and ceiling fans, and we install new devices on existing circuits where the wiring supports it. Anything that involves new circuits, panel work, or opening walls for new runs is coordinated with a licensed electrician — we are straightforward about that line and we do not cross it.

Older homes in Everett often have two-prong outlets, ungrounded boxes, or a light fixture hanging from a box that was never rated for the weight. Those are worth fixing properly rather than quickly. We check the box, the wiring and the breaker before installing anything, and we tell you when the honest answer is 'this needs an electrician'.

We also handle the carpentry and patching around electrical work — the hole from a moved fixture, the drywall patch after a new outlet — so the job finishes clean.

Electrical photos are not published. All permit-required electrical work is performed by a licensed electrician.

Frequently asked questions

Are you a licensed electrician?

No. We do minor device replacement and coordinate a licensed electrician for new circuits, panel work and anything requiring a permit. We will say so up front if your job needs one.

Can you add a new outlet?

If it is a like-for-like replacement on an existing circuit, usually yes. A brand-new outlet with a new run needs an electrician and often a permit.

Do you install ceiling fans?

Yes, when the existing box is fan-rated and the wiring supports it. If the box is not rated, we replace the box or bring in an electrician.
