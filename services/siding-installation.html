Siding

Siding repair and replacement

Siding that keeps the rain out of the wall — with the flashing and rot repair that most re-sides quietly skip.

In this climate, siding is a rain screen first and a finish second. The failures we see around Everett almost never start with the siding material itself — they start behind it, where the housewrap was lapped the wrong way, the flashing at a window was never installed, or the bottom course sat too close to the ground and wicked water up. Re-siding without fixing those is an expensive way to hide a problem for five years.

We replace damaged sections, repair rot in the sheathing and framing, and re-side whole walls or whole houses when the damage is widespread. Every re-side includes new weather-resistive barrier, properly lapped, and new flashing at windows, doors and the roof line. We check for soft sheathing with a probe and show you photos of what we find before the new siding goes on.

Fiber cement (James Hardie and similar) is the most common choice here for durability and fire resistance. Engineered wood looks warmer and paints well. Vinyl is the budget option — fine when installed with room to move, but it hides problems rather than solving them. We install all three and will tell you which one your house actually needs.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a full re-side take?

Two to four weeks for a typical house, depending on square footage, trim detail and weather. We do not start a re-side we cannot dry in.

Can you match my existing siding?

Often yes for fiber cement and vinyl, and close for painted wood. Sun fading means a perfect match is not always possible on an older wall; we will show you a sample before ordering.

Do I need a permit?

Many jurisdictions require one for a full re-side, and some HOAs require approval of material and color. We check before work starts.

Will you find rot behind the siding?

Sometimes. We price the visible scope, then stop and show you if we open a wall and find damage. No surprise charges without your approval.

What is a rain screen?

A gap behind the siding that lets water drain and the wall dry. It is the difference between siding that lasts thirty years here and siding that fails in ten.
