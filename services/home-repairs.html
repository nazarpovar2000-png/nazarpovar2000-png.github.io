Repairs

Home repairs and handyman work

The whole odd-job list knocked out in one visit — doors, drywall, trim, fixtures and the things that have been waiting for a year.

Most homes accumulate a list: a door that rubs, a towel bar pulled out of drywall, a cabinet door that will not close, a piece of trim that came loose, a light fixture that never got swapped. None of them justify a contractor on their own. All of them together are a day or two of work and a house that feels noticeably better.

We work through punch lists room by room, and we are honest about what is a repair and what is a symptom. A soft spot in the floor near the tub is not a flooring problem. A door that sticks every summer is usually a humidity and clearance issue, not a hinge issue. If a small job reveals something bigger, we stop, show you, and quote it before continuing.

We also handle rental turnover work, pre-sale punch lists, and the small carpentry and fixture jobs that come up after a remodel.

How it works

  1. Call or send the form. Tell us what you have in mind and roughly when you need it.
  2. Free walkthrough. We look at the actual conditions — not a photo — and measure.
  3. Written estimate. Itemized scope, materials, and a real start date. Usually within one business day.
  4. Build and walkthrough. Daily cleanup, and a final walk so you see exactly what was done before final payment.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a minimum charge?

Yes, a two-hour minimum for handyman visits. Most lists fit in that window; larger ones we quote as a day.

Do you supply materials?

Small consumables yes. For fixtures and specific parts we either pick them up with your approval or ask you to choose them first.

Can you fix water damage?

We repair the visible damage and the cause where it is carpentry-related — rot, subfloor, trim. If it involves a plumbing leak, we coordinate a plumber first so the repair does not fail twice.

Do you work on rentals?

Yes. We do turnover repairs and pre-sale punch lists, and can coordinate access with a property manager.
